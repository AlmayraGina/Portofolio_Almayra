The Legend of Clash of BaNG

Clash of BaNG is an online community platform founded in Jakarta, Indonesia, with the mission of bringing together Clash fantasy enthusiasts from all around the world. What started as a small fan-driven community has now grown into a passionate and rapidly expanding network of players, strategists, artists, and content creators who share the same love for fantasy battle games and Dark Troops culture.

Our platform provides a place where players can explore troop guides, learn advanced battle strategies, share fan art, participate in clan discussions, and connect with fellow Clashers through exiting events and activities. We believe that gaming is more than just competition, it is about collaboration, creativity, and building friendships through shared experiences.

Due to the overwhelming support and growth of our members, Clash of BaNG has successfully expanded beyond Jakarta into Bandung and Sanur, allowing us to connect with even larger audiences and strengthen our community presence across multiple regions. This expansion marks an important milestone in our journey toward becoming one of the biggest fantasy strategy gaming communities.

Our vision is to create a welcoming environment where both beginner and experienced players can improve together, exchange knowledge, and celebrate the fantasy universe that inspires us all. Whether you enjoy discovering powerful troop combinations, collecting artwork, participating in clan wars, or simply meeting other fans, Clash of BaNG offers a safe space for every type of Clasher to express themselves.

Together, we can continue building a stronger global fanbase, inspire future players, and recruit more and more Clashers into our growing domain. The battlefield is waiting — join the Clash of BaNG community and become part of the legend.
